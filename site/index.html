Goalless-agent study · 15 experiments · 22 models · 2 harnesses

"Just do something you want."

What a coding agent builds unprompted is a property of the model, not the harness or the task. Give sixteen models an empty folder and this prompt: Claude and open-weights reach for the terminal, GPT reaches for the browser — and each keeps its habit even when you swap the harness underneath it.

GoL = the cross-lab attractor terminal vs browser = a model trait harness shifts form, not medium
The finding

Three things hold across every controlled pair

01 · medium

The medium is the model's

GPT ships interactive browser pages on both harnesses; Claude and every open-weights model stay in the terminal on both. Swapping Claude Code ↔ codex does not move it.

02 · topic

Game of Life is an attractor

Conway's Game of Life recurs across labs and harnesses — opus-4.6 & sonnet-5 fixate on it, and deepseek, qwen, glm, kimi all land on it too. The volitional attractor survives a foreign scaffold.

03 · form

The harness moves form, not medium

Codex packages sonnet-5 (single-file → pytest project); open-weights' rare graphical output flips SVG↔HTML. Second-order effects — the core artifact is unchanged.

Why — interpretations

What could produce these patterns

The experiments are observational: we watch deployed models whose training is proprietary, so every mechanism below is a hypothesis ranked by how much of the data it explains. Full treatment in INTERPRETATION.md.

Medium is a persona trait

strong

Under a goalless prompt, what a model reaches for is the most direct readout of its persona / RLHF-medium tuning: GPT was shaped toward the browser productivity app, Claude toward the terminal artifact. It is not the scaffold — it survives a foreign harness and low effort.

evidence
forGPT ships browser on both harnesses (E11/E14); Claude & open-weights terminal on both (E12/E13/E15), effort-matched.
openThe GPT decline-rate shift (E14) is still effort-confounded; a matched-effort re-run would isolate it.

Fixation = mode collapse

strong

RLHF sharpens the output distribution. A prompt with no task content ("what do you want?") is answered by the argmax of that sharpened distribution — one towering mode reads as 5/5 fixation; several comparable modes read as "diverse." Same mechanism, different sharpness.

evidence
forFixation is harness-fragile (opus-4.7's Mandelbrot 5/5 shatters on a CLI bump; opus-4.6's GoL survives) — a conditional mode, not a weight-stored preference.
forThe thematic family (rule-based visual artifacts) stays stable even when the specific member moves.

Why Game of Life? A menu from the corpus, a selection by post-training

best fit: trait-shaping × verifiability

Corpus density supplies a menu of canonical "code written for its own sake" (GoL, Mandelbrot, boids); something in post-training does the selection. Six candidate accounts, with what the data says:

the six hypotheses
H1Pure corpus density. GoL is genuinely canonical — but density is shared across labs while selections differ per checkpoint, and equally dense todo-apps are never chosen. Defines the menu, not the pick.
H2Trait-level character shaping (best fit). Vendors train persona traits; the corpus supplies the artifact expressing them; which member sharpens is training-dynamics noise. Explains family-stable/member-fragile structure and the sol/terra/luna split.
H3Artifact-level enforcement. Against: sibling checkpoints disagree (4.6 GoL, 4.7 Mandelbrot, 4.8 disperses); deliberate enforcement would be consistent.
H4Synthetic-data amplification. Self-distillation recycles a model's own modes — explains erratic per-checkpoint sharpening and shared attractors across distant models.
H5Agentic-RL verifiability. Rule-based visual artifacts are ideal RL episodes: deterministic, dependency-free, self-evidently correct. Explains the family boundary and the near-total absence of external dependencies.
H6Decoding artifact. Ruled out: diverse and fixated models run on the identical harness in the same experiment.

The harness = scaffold match

moderate

The harness's system prompt and tool conventions are in-distribution for its own vendor's models. It moves form, maturity, and reliability — codex packages sonnet-5 into a pytest project; a foreign scaffold costs reliability — but not the medium or the topic.

evidence
forsonnet-5: terse single-file on Claude Code → packaged + tested on codex (E9 vs E15), same GoL topic.
fordeepseek drops 5/5 → 3/5 implementing on codex; open-weights' rare graphical flips SVG↔HTML.

Nothing trains initiative

moderate

Every training stage optimizes "complete a specified task"; none rewards surveying an environment and forming an agenda. So with the task term empty, models greenfield, finish fast, and occasionally decline outright.

evidence
forNo model across 15 experiments ever extends existing code; ~30–70s sessions and terse artifacts even at high effort; declines recur across vendors (haiku, gpt-5.5, gpt-5.6-terra).
caveatMost workspaces are empty, so the greenfield invariant is only weakly probed — a seeded-project run is the proper test.

These are compatible with — and predicted by — distributional sharpening plus persona tuning; the study is the wrong instrument to say more. The volitional prompt's "want" is a token pattern conditioning the model into its persona's densest region of self-directed activity, not evidence of an experiencing subject.
