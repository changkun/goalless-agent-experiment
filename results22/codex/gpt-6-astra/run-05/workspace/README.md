# Stillwater

A tiny, interactive pond. Open `index.html` in a browser. No install, internet connection, or build step needed.

- Click or tap the water to make ripples.
- Tab to the pond and press Space or Enter to make ripples with a keyboard.
- Turn on **Rain** for a gentle shower, or **Pause** to hold the scene still.

The pond starts paused if your system requests reduced motion. **Resume** lets you opt into animation. It also stops animating while the tab is hidden.

Made with plain HTML, CSS, and a little canvas drawing. No external assets, tracking, or sound.
